Letterbox distribution on the Sunshine Coast, done properly

Letterbox marketing still works on the Sunshine Coast. Trades, real estate agents, cafes, gyms, schools and community events all rely on it because a printed flyer in the hand is hard to ignore. The problem has never been the medium. It has been reliability. Flyers get dumped, runs get skipped, and the client never finds out.

Aura Print & Promo coordinates letterbox drops from Caloundra and Pelican Waters through Kawana, Buderim, Maroochydore and Nambour to Coolum, Peregian and Noosa. We print your flyer on the right stock for walkers, bundle it to the distributor's specification, and book the run with a team that can show where every walker went. Our preferred method is Australia Post Unaddressed Mail: we book the localities, bundle to Australia Post’s specification, lodge the job and your flyer is delivered by the regular postie in a fixed week. It costs more per letterbox than a walker, and it is the only option that reaches locked apartment blocks and rural roadside boxes, with no walker to skip a street.

Shared runs are the lowest cost per home and suit most local businesses. Solo runs put your flyer in the letterbox on its own and suit launches, real estate listings and time-sensitive offers. Tell us the goal and we will recommend which one, and whether category exclusivity is worth paying for.

What affects the price

Quantity, shared versus solo delivery, the number of separate suburbs, the weight of the item, timing, and whether Australia Post is needed for part of the run. A DL or A5 flyer on 150gsm is the most economical to both print and deliver. Heavier card and folded A4 cost more per home to walk.

Frequently asked

How long does a run take? Most Sunshine Coast runs complete within one to two weeks of the flyers arriving with the distributor. Allow three to five business days for printing before that.

Do walkers deliver to "no junk mail" letterboxes? No. Neither do posties. Your quantity is based on letterboxes that accept unaddressed material.

Can I choose only part of a suburb? Yes. Tell us the streets or estate in the notes and adjust the quantity.

What proof do I get? A completion report from the distributor. Where the team runs GPS tracking, you get the route data as well.
