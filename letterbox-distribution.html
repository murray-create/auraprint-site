Why Australia Post, not walkers

  • It actually gets delivered. A postie is doing a mail round they are paid to complete and audited on. There is no incentive to dump a bundle in a bin.
  • It reaches what walkers cannot. Locked apartment foyers, gated estates and rural roadside boxes are all part of the normal mail round.
  • A delivery week you can plan around. You nominate the week, so the flyer can land before an event, an opening or a sale.
  • Official counts, not estimates. Letterbox numbers come from Australia Post’s own delivery point file, so the quantity we quote is the quantity we lodge.
  • Print and drop as one job. Right stock, right weight band, bundled to Australia Post specification and lodged for you.
  • One local contact. We are at Baringa. You deal with us, not a call centre.

Letterbox distribution on the Sunshine Coast, done properly

Letterbox marketing still works on the Sunshine Coast. Trades, real estate agents, cafes, gyms, schools and community events all rely on it because a printed flyer in the hand is hard to ignore. The problem has never been the medium. It has been reliability. Flyers get dumped, runs get skipped, and the client never finds out.

Aura Print & Promo books letterbox drops from Beerwah and Caloundra through Kawana, Buderim, Maroochydore and Nambour to Coolum, Peregian, Noosa and the hinterland. Every run goes through Australia Post Unaddressed Mail. We book the localities, print the flyer on a stock that sits in the right weight band, bundle it to Australia Post specification, lodge the job and your flyer is delivered by the regular postie in the week you nominate.

It costs more per letterbox than a backpacker with a trolley. That is the point. A walker run is a promise that someone walked your street, and the only way you find out they did not is that nothing happens. An Australia Post run comes with a lodgement receipt, a delivery week, and a postie who is already contracted to visit every one of those letterboxes that day.

Across the Sunshine Coast there are 127,958 residential letterboxes and 8,903 business letterboxes that accept unaddressed advertising material. You can book any combination of them, from a single suburb to the whole coast. Most first campaigns start with one or two suburbs, measure the response, then scale the areas that worked.

What affects the price

Two things: Australia Post postage per article, and printing. Postage depends on the size of the article and its weight band. A DL or A5 flyer on 150gsm stays inside the small letter, under 50 gram band, which is the cheapest rate Australia Post charges. Go up to a full A4 or a heavy card and the rate steps up, so the stock you choose changes the delivery cost as well as the print cost. Tick your suburbs in the form above and the postage estimate updates from the live rate card.

Frequently asked

Why use Australia Post instead of a walker? Posties deliver your flyer with the day's mail, so it reaches locked apartment foyers, gated estates and rural roadside boxes that walkers cannot get to. You also get a guaranteed delivery week and a lodgement receipt, rather than a promise that a run happened.

How many letterboxes are there on the Sunshine Coast? There are 127,958 residential letterboxes and 8,903 business letterboxes across the Sunshine Coast that accept unaddressed advertising material, based on Australia Post delivery point data extracted on 25 August 2026. You can book any combination of suburbs from that total.

Do flyers go into no junk mail letterboxes? No. Letterboxes marked no junk mail or no advertising material are excluded from the counts and from the delivery, so you are never charged for them.

How long does a letterbox drop take? Allow three to five business days for printing, then the run is lodged for a nominated Australia Post delivery week. Bookings usually need to be lodged about two weeks ahead of that week.

Can I drop to only part of a suburb? Yes. Tell us the streets or the estate in the booking notes and we adjust the quantity. Australia Post delivery rounds are the smallest unit we can book below a whole locality.
