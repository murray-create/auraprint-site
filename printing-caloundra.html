Caloundra printing questions

Do you have a shop in Caloundra?

No. We are based in Baringa, in the same 4551 postcode, but there is no shop counter. We work by phone, email and online proof, and we deliver Caloundra jobs to you ourselves.

Which suburbs do you deliver to in person?

Caloundra and the surrounding 4551 suburbs, including Caloundra West, Little Mountain, Pelican Waters, Golden Beach, Currimundi, Baringa and Nirimba. Jobs for the rest of the Sunshine Coast go by courier, and so do jobs anywhere else in Australia.

Can I see my job before it is printed?

Yes. You approve a proof before anything goes to press. If something looks wrong on the proof, we fix it and send it again.

Can you help with my artwork?

Yes. We check every file before it prints, and we can set up or design your artwork if you do not have it ready. Our artwork guide explains bleed, colour and resolution if you are preparing it yourself.

How quickly can I have it?

It depends on the product and how busy the presses are. Ask us before you order and we will give you an honest date. Faster options are sometimes possible on business cards and flyers.

Can you letterbox my flyers in Caloundra too?

Yes. We print the flyers and book them with Australia Post, who deliver them to letterboxes across the Caloundra suburbs you choose. Our Caloundra letterbox page shows the official letterbox counts.
