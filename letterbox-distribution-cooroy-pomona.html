Letterbox drops in Cooroy & Pomona

The northern hinterland behind Noosa. Cooroy and Pomona are proper towns with their own main streets, and around them sit Cooran, Kin Kin, Black Mountain and the Lake Macdonald acreage belt.

The Cooroy & Pomona area is 13 suburbs and 5,006 residential letterboxes, which is about 4% of every letterbox on the Sunshine Coast. Cooroy is the largest single suburb in it at 1,187 letterboxes, so it is usually where a first test drop goes.

Who drops flyers here

Rural suppliers and machinery, cafes and galleries on Maple Street and Factory Street, trades and land services, schools, and tourism and accommodation operators.

Why we book it through Australia Post

This is a long way from any coastal depot and it shows in the coverage most distributors offer. Australia Post runs the mail here every day, so what is a difficult run for everyone else is an ordinary one for a postie.

Every run is lodged as Australia Post Unaddressed Mail. You nominate a delivery week, we print the flyer on a stock that sits in the right weight band, bundle it to Australia Post specification, lodge the job and you get the lodgement receipt. There is no walker to skip a street and no report to take on trust.

Sizing the run

Cooroy, Pomona and Cooran together are close to 3,000 homes, and they are rarely targeted. Competition for attention in that letterbox is low.

Frequently asked

How many letterboxes are there in Cooroy & Pomona? There are 5,006 residential letterboxes and 304 business letterboxes across the 13 suburbs in the Cooroy & Pomona area that accept unaddressed advertising material. The largest single suburb is Cooroy with 1,187 residential letterboxes. These are Australia Post delivery point counts, extracted 25 August 2026.

What does a letterbox drop across Cooroy & Pomona cost? Covering every residential letterbox in the area is 5,006 letterboxes. At 47.6 cents per letterbox delivered, GST included, that is about $2,385. Printing is quoted on top. Delivery-only jobs carry a minimum charge of $250. It is waived when we print the flyer as well. You do not have to take the whole area: pick the suburbs you want and the quantity follows.

Can I book just one suburb in Cooroy & Pomona? Yes. Australia Post lets us book by locality, so you can take a single suburb or any combination of them. For a smaller area than that, tell us the streets or the estate in the booking notes and we size the run to match.

How long does it take? Allow three to five business days to print, then the run is lodged for a nominated Australia Post delivery week. Bookings generally need to be in about two weeks ahead of the week you want.

Not your area?

We book Australia Post Unaddressed Mail to any postcode in Australia, not just the Sunshine Coast. Same posties, same delivery week, same official letterbox counts. Tell us the suburbs or the postcodes and we will come back with the numbers.
