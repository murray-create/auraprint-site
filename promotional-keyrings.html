What branded keyrings cost

A real worked example

150 floating keychains with a one colour print come to $270 plus GST, which is $1.80 each including setup. Metal keyrings with engraving usually land between $2 and $6 each at 100 units. Leather and PU keyrings sit higher again. Every keyring here has its own calculator.

Metal, plastic, leather or cork

Metal keyrings feel substantial and take laser engraving, which is permanent. Plastic and acrylic keyrings are cheaper and print in full colour, so a detailed logo works. Leather and PU read as a premium gift. Cork and recycled options carry an eco flag, and floating keyrings are genuinely useful on the Coast if your customers are anywhere near a boat.

Print or engrave

Engraving on metal is permanent and looks expensive, but it shows the bare metal rather than your brand colour. Printing keeps your colours and costs about the same, but it will wear over years of rattling around in a pocket with coins and keys. On a keyring specifically, engraving usually wins because of what the item goes through.

Bottle openers and multi-tools

A large slice of the keyring range doubles as something else, usually a bottle opener, a trolley token or a small multi-tool. These get a better response than a plain tag because they earn their place on the keys. They cost a little more per unit and the decoration area is smaller, so keep the logo simple.

What the Coast uses branded keyrings for

Motor dealers and mechanics

Handed over with the keys at delivery or service. The single most natural fit for a keyring there is.

Real estate

Settlement gifts and rental handovers. The keyring goes on the keys of the house you just sold them and stays there.

Tourism and accommodation

Room keys, hire gear and gift shop stock. Floating keyrings sell in coastal towns because they solve a real problem.

Clubs and events

Bottle opener keyrings at member sign-ups and presentation nights. Cheap, useful and kept.

Trades

Left with a customer after a job, alongside a business card magnet on the fridge.

Schools and fundraising

Full colour acrylic keyrings printed with student artwork, which sell well at fetes and cost very little to make.

Artwork for promotional products

Send a vector file: EPS, AI or PDF with the fonts outlined. A JPG or PNG taken off a website cannot be screen printed or engraved cleanly at product size. Send your brand colours as Pantone references too, because decoration is colour matched to Pantone rather than CMYK, and a colour that looks right on screen will not necessarily mix the same in ink.

Decoration areas are smaller than people expect. If your logo has fine detail, small type or a tagline, it may need simplifying to survive at 35mm wide. We will tell you before you commit, and you will see it on the mockup at actual size. If all you have is a low resolution logo, say so and we will redraw it.

Frequently asked questions

How much are 100 branded keyrings?

Usually $150 to $400 plus GST at 100 units depending on the material. Plastic and acrylic sit at the low end, metal in the middle, leather and multi-tools at the top. Use the calculator on each product for a real number.

Should I print or engrave a metal keyring?

Engrave. A keyring lives in a pocket with keys and coins, and printed ink wears off eventually. Engraving is permanent. The trade-off is that your logo shows as bare metal instead of your brand colour.

Can I get a full colour keyring?

Yes, on acrylic and printed plastic lines. That is the way to go if your logo has gradients, a photo or several colours. Metal engraving cannot do colour.

What is the minimum order on keyrings?

Usually 100 units, sometimes 50 on higher value lines. The first break is shown on each product.

Do you have floating keyrings?

Yes. They are popular on the Sunshine Coast for obvious reasons, and they suit marine businesses, boat hire, tourism operators and anyone whose customers spend time on the water.

How long do branded keyrings take?

Around seven to ten working days for standard production once artwork is approved, plus freight.

What artwork do you need?

A vector file, so EPS, AI or PDF with fonts outlined. Keyring decoration areas are small, so a logo with fine detail or small text may need simplifying. We will show you a mockup at actual size before anything is made.
