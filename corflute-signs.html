CORFLUTE SIGNS SUNSHINE COAST

Real estate, builders and event boards on 3mm or 5mm corflute, any size.

See your corflute price before you enquire

Most Sunshine Coast sign suppliers make you fill in a form and wait. The panel above prices your corflute the moment you pick a size, a thickness and how many you need, with GST and standard Australia-wide delivery already in the number.

Corflute is the cheapest way to put a big, weatherproof message in front of people. It is a fluted polypropylene board, so it will not soak up rain, warp or rot the way a printed board on ply or MDF does. We print it with UV inks that hold their colour through a Queensland summer.

3mm or 5mm corflute?

3mm is the standard real estate and event board. It is light, it cable-ties to a fence in seconds, and it is the cheapest per sign. Use it for anything that comes down inside six months, or anything you will be moving around.

5mm is noticeably stiffer. Choose it for boards over about 900mm on the long edge, for anything screwed to a frame or a star picket, and for signs that will sit in a windy spot. The price difference is small, often only a few dollars a sign, and a floppy board in a coastal breeze looks cheap no matter how good the artwork is.

Sizes we hold as standard

600 x 450mm, 600 x 900mm, 900 x 600mm, 900 x 1200mm and 1200 x 900mm all price instantly in the calculator. Anything outside that, including full 2400 x 1200 sheets and odd shapes, we quote. Ask and we will come back with a number, usually the same business day.

Single sided or double sided

If people only approach your sign from one direction, print one side. If it sits on a corner, between two lanes of traffic, or on a freestanding frame, print both. Double sided is not double the price because the board and the handling are the same.

What the Coast uses corflute for

Real estate

For Sale, Sold, Open Home, Auction Today and Under Offer boards. Agents from Caloundra through to Noosa order these in tens, because a board that lasts one campaign has done its job.

Builders and trades

Site identification, safety notices and lot signage. There is a lot of it going up right now across Aura, Baringa, Nirimba, Palmview and Harmony, and corflute survives a build far better than a printed board.

Schools and community groups

Fete and open day signs, enrolment boards, working bee notices, sponsor boards on a fence. Cheap enough to print a set, tough enough to store and reuse next year.

Events and markets

Directional signage, stallholder boards and parking arrows for events at Caloundra, Mooloolaba, Kawana and Noosa. Light enough to carry in a car boot.

Elections and campaigns

The classic corflute job. Booth signs, fence signs and A-frame inserts, printed in volume so the per-sign cost drops right down.

Cafes, gyms and shopfronts

Now Open, Now Hiring, promotions and seasonal offers. Change the message as often as you like at this price point.

Frequently asked questions

How long do corflute signs last outside?

12 to 24 months in Queensland sun. We print with UV inks that resist fading, and the board itself is polypropylene so rain does not affect it. Signs in full afternoon sun age faster than signs in shade, and darker colours show fading before lighter ones.

How much do corflute signs cost?

A single 900 x 600mm sign in 5mm is $148 including GST and delivery. Ten of the same sign are $245, which is $24.50 each, and one hundred are $1,262, which is $12.62 each. Smaller 600 x 450mm boards start from $140. Setup is most of the cost, so quantity brings the per-sign price down sharply.

Should I choose 3mm or 5mm corflute?

Use 3mm for standard real estate and event boards that cable-tie to a fence. Use 5mm for anything longer than about 900mm, anything mounted to a frame or star picket, and anything in a windy or exposed position. The price difference is only a few dollars a sign.

What sizes do you print?

600 x 450mm, 600 x 900mm, 900 x 600mm, 900 x 1200mm and 1200 x 900mm all price instantly on this page. We also print full 2400 x 1200 sheets and custom shapes, which we quote on request.

How quickly can I get corflute signs on the Sunshine Coast?

Standard production is 3 to 5 business days from artwork approval, then delivery. Every effort is made to get your job onto the press for the dispatch shown. On the odd occasion a press or freight delay pushes it out, and we will tell you as soon as we know.

Can you deliver to my address on the Sunshine Coast?

Yes. Signs go by courier Australia-wide, and local Sunshine Coast deliveries we often run out ourselves. We do not offer pickup from Baringa.

Do the signs come with eyelets?

Eyelets are available on request rather than by default. Tell us where you want them and mark the positions on your artwork file. Most fence-mounted signs use four corner eyelets and cable ties.

Can you design the sign for me?

Yes. Send your logo, your text and any photos, and we will lay it out and send you a proof. If you would rather set it up yourself, download one of our free artwork templates, which have the bleed, trim and safe area already marked.

What file do you need?

A print-ready PDF in CMYK at 150dpi final size with 10mm bleed, fonts outlined. We preflight every file by hand and will come back to you if anything is going to cause a problem, before it prints.

Will I see a proof before it prints?

Always. A person checks your artwork, then you get a digital proof to approve. Nothing goes on press until you say yes.

Are corflute signs recyclable?

Corflute is polypropylene, plastic type 5, and is recyclable where your local facility accepts it. It is not accepted in most kerbside bins on the Sunshine Coast, so check with your council or drop it at a commercial recycler.

Can I print the same sign in two different messages?

Yes, but each different artwork is treated as its own run because the press has to be set up again. If you need ten Open Home and ten Auction Today, ask us to quote it as one job and we will tell you where the break-even sits.
