Letterbox drops in Eumundi & Yandina

The Maroochy River flats and the country between Nambour and Noosa. Yandina is the working town, Eumundi trades on its markets, and Doonan is large lot acreage feeding into the Noosa hinterland.

The Eumundi & Yandina area is 13 suburbs and 5,920 residential letterboxes, which is about 5% of every letterbox on the Sunshine Coast. Doonan is the largest single suburb in it at 1,128 letterboxes, so it is usually where a first test drop goes.

Who drops flyers here

Markets and food producers, rural and equine services, tradespeople, accommodation and wedding venues, and local trades servicing the acreage blocks.

Why we book it through Australia Post

Almost everything outside the Yandina and Eumundi town blocks is roadside delivery on rural routes. Those addresses are real households with real money, and they are the first thing a walker run drops when the schedule gets tight.

Every run is lodged as Australia Post Unaddressed Mail. You nominate a delivery week, we print the flyer on a stock that sits in the right weight band, bundle it to Australia Post specification, lodge the job and you get the lodgement receipt. There is no walker to skip a street and no report to take on trust.

Sizing the run

Doonan and Verrierdale are effectively Noosa hinterland. If you are targeting Noosa money without Noosa prices, include them.

Frequently asked

How many letterboxes are there in Eumundi & Yandina? There are 5,920 residential letterboxes and 220 business letterboxes across the 13 suburbs in the Eumundi & Yandina area that accept unaddressed advertising material. The largest single suburb is Doonan with 1,128 residential letterboxes. These are Australia Post delivery point counts, extracted 25 August 2026.

What does a letterbox drop across Eumundi & Yandina cost? Covering every residential letterbox in the area is 5,920 letterboxes. At 47.6 cents per letterbox delivered, GST included, that is about $2,821. Printing is quoted on top. Delivery-only jobs carry a minimum charge of $250. It is waived when we print the flyer as well. You do not have to take the whole area: pick the suburbs you want and the quantity follows.

Can I book just one suburb in Eumundi & Yandina? Yes. Australia Post lets us book by locality, so you can take a single suburb or any combination of them. For a smaller area than that, tell us the streets or the estate in the booking notes and we size the run to match.

How long does it take? Allow three to five business days to print, then the run is lodged for a nominated Australia Post delivery week. Bookings generally need to be in about two weeks ahead of the week you want.

Not your area?

We book Australia Post Unaddressed Mail to any postcode in Australia, not just the Sunshine Coast. Same posties, same delivery week, same official letterbox counts. Tell us the suburbs or the postcodes and we will come back with the numbers.
