About this style

The Gildan 12500 Premium T-Shirt offers a step up from the standard, delivering superior comfort without compromising on durability. Made from 100% ring-spun cotton, this T-shirt provides a soft, smooth feel that holds its shape wash after wash. The premium fabric ensures longevity, while the classic fit offers room for easy movement, making it an excellent choice for a variety of applications. Ideal for DTG, heat transfer and screen print. Recommended for sublimation and embroidery.

  • Fabric: 305g/m² | 50% Cotton / 50% Polyester
  • Fit: Adult Hooded Sweatshirt
  • Sizes: S, M, L, XL, 2XL
  • Colours: 2
Features
  • Fabric weight: 305g/m²
  • Fabric composition: 50% ring spun cotton, 50% polyester
  • 100% of our fabric cutting scraps are recycled into fiber and used in new products
  • 1x1 rib with spandex cuffs and bottom band for enhanced stretch and recovery
  • 33% of the energy used to manufacture our products comes from renewable resources
  • Classic fit tubular body
  • Classic midweight fabric with DryBlend technology: delivers moisture-wicking properties
  • Double-lined hood with colour-matched drawcord
  • Double-needle pouch pocket for added reinforcement
  • Double-needle stitching at shoulders, armholes, neck, waistband and cuffs
Adding your logo

Embroidery from $15.50 inc GST per garment, plus a one-off digitising fee of $68 inc GST per logo. DTF suits small runs and full colour logos. Screen print is best value from about 25 pieces per design. We confirm the decoration price with your quote.
