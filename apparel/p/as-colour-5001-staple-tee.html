About this style

The AS Colour Staple Tee. Enduring comfort in a regular fit, crafted from 180 GSM 100% combed cotton. Featuring neck ribbing, side seams, and double needle hems for durability and minimal shrinkage, it's a timeless choice for comfort and style. Available in 70+ colours. Built to last.

  • Fabric: 100% Combed Cotton (Marles 15% Viscose), 180 GSM
  • Fit: Regular Fit - 180 GSM
  • Sizes: S, M, L, XL, 2XL, 3XL, 4XL, 5XL, XSM
  • Colours: 76
  • Carton quantity: 72
Adding your logo

Embroidery from $15.50 inc GST per garment, plus a one-off digitising fee of $68 inc GST per logo. DTF suits small runs and full colour logos. Screen print is best value from about 25 pieces per design. We confirm the decoration price with your quote.
