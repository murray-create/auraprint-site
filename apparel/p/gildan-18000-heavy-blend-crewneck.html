About this style

Step into a sweatshirt that's as versatile as your lifestyle. The Gildan Heavyblend 18000 Crewneck Sweatshirt isn't just a wardrobe staple, it's the piece you'll reach for when you want to feel effortlessly put together. With a blend of durability and softness, it adapts to your day, whether you're heading out or cozying up at home. Choose from a range of rich colors to express your style, and enjoy the kind of comfort that feels uniquely yours. Perfect for layering or standing out on its own, this pullover fleece is all about making every day a little more you. Ideal for embroidery, heat transfer and screen print and recommended for DTG and sublimation.

  • Fabric: 271g/m² | 50% Cotton / 50% Polyester
  • Fit: Adult Crewneck Sweatshirt
  • Sizes: XS, S, M, L, XL, 2XL, 3XL, 4XL, 5XL
  • Colours: 20
Features
  • Fabric weight: 271g/m²
  • Fabric composition: 50% ring spun cotton, 50% polyester
  • 100% of our fabric cutting scraps are recycled into fiber and used in new products
  • 1x1 rib with spandex for enhanced stretch and recovery
  • 33% of the energy used to manufacture our products comes from renewable resources
  • Classic fit tubular body
  • Double-needle stitching at shoulders, armholes, neck, waistband and cuffs
  • Tear away label
  • Spun yarn for softer feel and reduced pilling
  • *Heather Colours - 60% Polyester, 40% Cotton
Adding your logo

Embroidery from $15.50 inc GST per garment, plus a one-off digitising fee of $68 inc GST per logo. DTF suits small runs and full colour logos. Screen print is best value from about 25 pieces per design. We confirm the decoration price with your quote.
