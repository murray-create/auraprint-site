About this style

Our unique pigment dye process has less impact on the environment. This technique creates a garment that's cozy from the second you put it on and keeps getting softer every time you wear it.

  • Fabric: 207g/m² | 100% Ring Spun US Cotton
  • Fit: Heavyweight Youth T-Shirt
  • Sizes: XS, S, M, L, XL
  • Colours: 7
Features
  • Fabric weight: 207g/ ㎡
  • Fabric composition: 100% ring spun US cotton
  • Pre-shrunk during the garment dye process for almost no shrinkage at home
  • Our clean pigment dye colours use 50% less water than traditional pigment dye processes
  • Garment-dyed soft ring spun fabric feels great against your skin
  • Double-needle topstitched, classic width collar
  • Twill taped neck and shoulders for comfort and durability
  • Classic fit, tubular body
  • Signature twill label and shoulder to shoulder twill tape
  • CPSIA Tracking label compliant
