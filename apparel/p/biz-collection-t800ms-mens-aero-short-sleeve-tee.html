About this style

Perform any activity in comfort in our light-as-air AERO breathable tee. This is functional simplicity at its best with no-fuss anti-shrink, anti-fade fabric and a cotton-like feel that keeps you cool and dry. A heathered marle fabric and overlocked seam stitching delivers a modern design element to this classic style.

  • Fabric: 100% BIZ COOL™ Cotton-feel Micro Polyester, 160 GSM, UPF rating - Good
  • Fit: Modern Fit
  • Sizes: XS, S, M, L, XL, 2XL, 3XL, 5XL
  • Colours: 5
