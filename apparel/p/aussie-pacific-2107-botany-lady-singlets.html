About this style

A uniform staple. The Botany Singlet is focused on value and versatility. Simple, plain design in our tried and tested mini waffle fabric. The botany is equally suited to sporting field or large event merchandise. Available in 9 colours and at a price that will certainly guarantee value for you and your customers

  • Fabric: 160gm 100% Polyester
  • Sizes: 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, 20

Features
  • Mini waffle knit Dri-wear antibacterial finish Easy care fabric Self fabric bind on armhole and neck V Neck self fabric neck bind (ladies' only)
Adding your logo

Embroidery from $15.50 inc GST per garment, plus a one-off digitising fee of $68 inc GST per logo. DTF suits small runs and full colour logos. Screen print is best value from about 25 pieces per design. We confirm the decoration price with your quote.
