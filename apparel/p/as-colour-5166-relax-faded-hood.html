About this style

Experience the AS Colour Relax Faded Hood, offering a relaxed fit in mid-weight 320 GSM fabric, 80% cotton and 20% recycled polyester fleece. Features include a pullover style, drop shoulder, kangaroo pocket, self-fabric lined hood, and garment dyed finish. Complete with sleeve cuff ribbing and a tear-out label for simplicity. Saving approx. 7 500ml plastic bottles from landfill.

  • Fabric: 80% Cotton 20% Recycled Polyester Fleece, 320 GSM
  • Fit: Relaxed Fit - 320 GSM
  • Sizes: XS, S, M, L, XL, 2XL, 3XL
  • Colours: 6
  • Carton quantity: 10
Adding your logo

Embroidery from $15.50 inc GST per garment, plus a one-off digitising fee of $68 inc GST per logo. DTF suits small runs and full colour logos. Screen print is best value from about 25 pieces per design. We confirm the decoration price with your quote.
