FLOOR STICKERS SUNSHINE COAST

Anti-slip round floor graphics for shops, cafés, gyms, car parks and events. Pick a size and material and the price is on screen, GST and delivery included.

Floor graphics that stay down and stay legible

A floor sticker works because people are already looking at the floor. Arrows, queue markers, keep-left messages, safety warnings and promotions all get read at walking pace without anyone having to look up. Pick a size and material above and the price is on screen with GST and standard Australia-wide delivery included.

The material is the decision that matters. A floor graphic lives under shoes, trolleys and mops, so the top laminate needs the right slip rating and the adhesive needs to suit the surface. We offer four rated materials so you are not paying for an outdoor product on a shop floor, or putting an indoor product on a car park where it will lift in a week.

Which material?

  • Avery Economy, R9, about 3 months. Smooth, sealed indoor floors: retail, offices, foyers. The choice for a short campaign, a sale or a temporary event layout.
  • MyFloor Economy, R9, about 6 months. The same indoor use with a longer life. Best value for directional markers that need to stay down for a season.
  • TrafficTex Outdoor, R11, about 6 months. Sealed outdoor and heavy traffic surfaces: polished concrete, tiles under cover, busy entries where the floor gets wet.
  • Asphalt Art Nuevo, R13, up to 9 months. Textured surfaces: rough concrete, pavers, bitumen car parks and footpaths. It presses into the texture, so it does not lift at the edges the way a smooth film would.

Which size?

150mm is a spot marker: footprints, small arrows, a dot on a queue line. 300mm is the standard size for a directional or safety message and is what most shops and venues order. 400mm is for a logo, a promotional message or anything that has to read from across a room. Custom shapes and larger sizes are available on request.

Getting them to stay down

Most floor stickers that fail were put onto a floor that was polished, dusty or still damp. Clean the area, let it dry completely, then press from the centre outwards. On outdoor textured surfaces the Asphalt Art material is worked in with a roller or stiff brush. Done properly, the sticker lasts its rated life and comes up cleanly when you are finished with it.

Running a promotion across the whole store? Pair floor graphics with an A-frame at the door, posters and window and product stickers so the message is the same from the footpath to the counter.

Where floor stickers earn their keep

Retail and supermarkets

Directional arrows, queue spacing and end-of-aisle promotions. The 300mm round on MyFloor is the standard order.

Cafés and hospitality

Order here, collect here, and mind-the-step warnings near a kitchen pass. Wipe-clean laminate copes with mopping.

Gyms and studios

Station markers, spacing dots and class layouts that survive sweat, weights and daily cleaning.

Events and expos

Footprint trails from the entry to your stand, sponsor logos on the concourse, and wayfinding that comes up cleanly afterwards.

Car parks and footpaths

Keep-clear zones, pedestrian arrows and branding on bitumen or pavers. This is what the R13 Asphalt Art material is for.

Warehouses and safety

Walkway edges, forklift zones and hazard warnings where the floor is the safest place to put the message.

Frequently asked questions

What do floor stickers cost?

Pricing starts at $187 including GST and standard Australia-wide delivery, and the configurator above shows the price for your exact size, material and quantity. The minimum order is small on purpose, so a set of ten arrows for a shop is realistic.

Which material should I choose?

Match the material to the floor and how long the sticker has to last. The Avery Economy R9 suits a smooth indoor floor for about three months, the MyFloor Economy R9 stretches that to about six months indoors. TrafficTex Outdoor R11 is the pick for sealed outdoor surfaces and busy entries for around six months. Asphalt Art Nuevo R13 is textured and rated for rough surfaces like concrete, pavers and bitumen for up to nine months.

What do the R9, R11 and R13 slip ratings mean?

They are the Australian pendulum slip ratings for the top surface. R9 is a standard indoor rating for dry retail and office floors. R11 is suitable where floors can get wet or see heavy foot traffic. R13 is the highest and is meant for outdoor, textured or wet surfaces. Pick the rating that matches where the sticker is going, not the cheapest one.

Can I put them outdoors?

Yes, on the right material. The TrafficTex R11 is made for sealed outdoor surfaces such as polished concrete and tiles under cover. The Asphalt Art R13 is the one for rough concrete, pavers, bitumen car parks and footpaths, because it conforms to the texture and will not lift at the edges.

How long do they last?

Each material is rated for a period of normal foot traffic: three months for Avery Economy, six months for MyFloor and TrafficTex, and nine months for Asphalt Art. Trolleys, forklifts, wet mopping with harsh chemicals and direct sun all shorten that. Treat the rating as a guide for a clean, well prepared floor.

How do I apply them?

The floor must be clean, dry and free of dust, wax and polish. Peel the backing, position the sticker, then press firmly from the centre outwards to push out air. On textured outdoor surfaces the Asphalt Art material is pressed in with a roller or a stiff brush so it keys into the surface. Do not apply to a floor that is still damp from mopping.

Will they leave residue when I take them up?

The indoor materials are designed to remove cleanly from sealed floors within their rated life. Any adhesive left behind on a smooth floor comes off with warm soapy water. The outdoor Asphalt Art material is meant to wear away rather than be peeled up in one piece.

What sizes are available?

Three round sizes: 150mm, 300mm and 400mm diameter. The 150mm suits arrows, footprints and small spot markers. The 300mm is the standard size for a directional or safety message that reads at walking pace. The 400mm is for a logo, a promotion or a message that has to be read from across a room. Need a square, a rectangle or a custom shape? Ask us for a quote.

What artwork do you need?

A print-ready PDF in CMYK with fonts outlined and 3mm bleed. Keep type large and bold, because a floor graphic is read at a glance from standing height, and remember it gets scuffed. Send us your logo and the message and we will lay it out and send a proof if you prefer.

Do you deliver Australia-wide?

Yes, and standard delivery is included in the price shown. We are based at Baringa on the Sunshine Coast and often drop local orders ourselves.
