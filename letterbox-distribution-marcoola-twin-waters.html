Letterbox drops in Bli Bli, Twin Waters & Marcoola

This is the airport corridor. Bli Bli has grown hard over the last decade and is now one of the larger family suburbs on the coast, while Twin Waters, Mudjimba and Marcoola stay smaller, older and closer to the beach.

The Bli Bli, Twin Waters & Marcoola area is 5 suburbs and 6,735 residential letterboxes, which is about 5% of every letterbox on the Sunshine Coast. Bli Bli is the largest single suburb in it at 2,803 letterboxes, so it is usually where a first test drop goes.

Who drops flyers here

Trades and pool services working the Bli Bli estates, cafes at Pacific Paradise and Marcoola, airport related businesses, and holiday accommodation operators.

Why we book it through Australia Post

Twin Waters is a gated style estate with its own internal road network, and Marcoola has a long run of beachfront units. Both are areas where an Australia Post booking gets you inside and a walker run gets you a stack at the entrance.

Every run is lodged as Australia Post Unaddressed Mail. You nominate a delivery week, we print the flyer on a stock that sits in the right weight band, bundle it to Australia Post specification, lodge the job and you get the lodgement receipt. There is no walker to skip a street and no report to take on trust.

Frequently asked

How many letterboxes are there in Bli Bli, Twin Waters & Marcoola? There are 6,735 residential letterboxes and 322 business letterboxes across the 5 suburbs in the Bli Bli, Twin Waters & Marcoola area that accept unaddressed advertising material. The largest single suburb is Bli Bli with 2,803 residential letterboxes. These are Australia Post delivery point counts, extracted 25 August 2026.

What does a letterbox drop across Bli Bli & Twin Waters cost? Covering every residential letterbox in the area is 6,735 letterboxes. At 47.6 cents per letterbox delivered, GST included, that is about $3,209. Printing is quoted on top. Delivery-only jobs carry a minimum charge of $250. It is waived when we print the flyer as well. You do not have to take the whole area: pick the suburbs you want and the quantity follows.

Can I book just one suburb in Bli Bli & Twin Waters? Yes. Australia Post lets us book by locality, so you can take a single suburb or any combination of them. For a smaller area than that, tell us the streets or the estate in the booking notes and we size the run to match.

How long does it take? Allow three to five business days to print, then the run is lodged for a nominated Australia Post delivery week. Bookings generally need to be in about two weeks ahead of the week you want.

Not your area?

We book Australia Post Unaddressed Mail to any postcode in Australia, not just the Sunshine Coast. Same posties, same delivery week, same official letterbox counts. Tell us the suburbs or the postcodes and we will come back with the numbers.
