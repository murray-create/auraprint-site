CROWD CONTROL BARRIERS & BANNERS

Branded barriers for queues, entries and event areas. A frame with a printed fabric panel, in three widths, priced on screen. Sunshine Coast owned, delivered Australia-wide.

Artwork setup specs

  • ▸ Sizes: 1000, 1500 or 2000mm wide, 900mm high
  • ▸ Options: frame and fabric, frame only or replacement fabric
  • ▸ Print: single or double sided

New to print files? Read the artwork setup guide for bleed, CMYK and 300dpi.

Need an artwork template? Ask through the quote form and we'll send one.

Why print with Aura Print?

Every job is preflight-checked by a human, and nothing prints until you approve it. You get a digital proof unless you tell us your file is print ready. Delivered Australia-wide, with local Sunshine Coast drops handled by us where we can.

Barriers that do the job and carry your brand

A crowd control barrier keeps a queue in line and an area closed off. A printed one does that and puts your logo in front of everyone waiting. Choose a width, what you need and how many sides are printed, and the price is on screen including GST.

Three widths, one height

Every barrier is 900mm high, about waist height, which is the height people expect a barrier to be. Choose 1000mm for doorways and tight spaces, 1500mm for most queues, and 2000mm to close off a wide area with fewer pieces. Stand several side by side to line a queue or fence off a zone.

What to order

  • Frame and fabric is the complete barrier. Start here the first time.
  • Replacement fabric fits a frame you already own. Use it to rebrand for a new event or sponsor without buying the frame again.
  • Frame only adds more frames to your set.

One side or two?

If people can see the barrier from both sides, print both. A queue at an event entry, a bar or a stage area is usually seen from both directions.

Designing the panel

  • Keep the logo high. People stand in front of a barrier, so the bottom of the panel gets blocked.
  • Use few words. A name, a logo and one message read from across a room.
  • Repeat across a set. When barriers stand in a row, the same design on each looks deliberate and tidy.

Where they are used

Event entries, VIP and media areas, bars, ticketing queues, sporting events, school and club days, and retail openings. Pair them with media walls and pull up banners for a complete event setup.

Frequently asked questions

What sizes are available?

Three widths: 1000mm, 1500mm and 2000mm, all 900mm high.

Can I reuse the frame?

Yes. Order replacement fabric to rebrand the barrier for a new event or sponsor, and keep using the frame you already have.

Should I print one side or two?

Print both sides if people will see the barrier from both directions, which is common at event entries, bars and stage areas.

Can I use them outdoors?

Yes, but a printed panel catches the wind. Outdoors, weight or secure the barriers so they cannot blow over.

How much do they cost?

A frame on its own starts at $146 and a complete 1000mm barrier with single sided fabric is $266, including GST. Choose your size and options in the price tool for an exact price.

Do you set them up?

No. The barriers are designed for you to set up and pack down yourself.

Do I see a proof before it prints?

Yes. Every file is checked by a person, and nothing prints until you approve the proof. If your file is print ready and you do not need a proof, just tell us.

Do you deliver Australia-wide?

Yes. We are based at Baringa on the Sunshine Coast and send jobs anywhere in Australia. If you are just up the road, we will often run it over ourselves.
