How ordering works

Price it online

We confirm live stock, the exact decoration cost for your logo and the freight to your address.

Frequently asked questions

Do you supply promotional products in Caloundra?

Yes. We are based in Baringa at Caloundra West, and we supply promotional products to businesses, clubs, schools and churches across the Caloundra area. We deliver Caloundra orders ourselves.

Do you have a showroom in Caloundra?

No. We sell online and we do not have a shop front or a pickup counter. We meet customers face to face when a job needs it, and we deliver Caloundra orders ourselves.

How much do promotional products cost?

It depends on the product, the decoration and the quantity. As worked examples from our catalogue today: $582 + GST for 250 × Capri Pen, laser engraving, setup included. Or $536 + GST for 125 × Recycled Colourful Cotton Tote Bag, screen print, setup included. Every product page shows its own price breaks.

What is the minimum order?

It depends on the product. Many items start at 25, 50 or 100 units. Each product page shows its price breaks, so you can see the smallest quantity before you ask.

Will I see my logo on the product first?

Yes. You get a mockup with your logo in position and at size. Nothing is decorated until you approve it.

What artwork do you need?

A vector file is best, such as an EPS, AI or PDF with the fonts outlined. If you only have a JPG or PNG, tell us early. We can redraw a logo as a vector file for a small fee.

How long does an order take?

It depends on the product, the decoration and the stock. We confirm the delivery date when we quote. If you have an event date, tell us up front.

Ready to put your logo on something?

Tell us the product, the quantity and your deadline. We will come back with a firm price, including freight to your address.
