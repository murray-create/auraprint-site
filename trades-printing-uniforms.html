Frequently asked questions

Is your hi vis compliant?

Compliance is set by the garment maker, not by us. Each hi vis style in our shop shows the standard its manufacturer publishes for it, such as day only or day and night use. Check that against what your site or principal contractor requires before you order, and ask us if you are unsure.

Can you put our logo on hi vis?

Yes. Embroidery is the usual choice, from $15.50 a garment inc GST, with a one off digitising fee of $68 inc GST per logo. We keep the logo clear of the reflective tape, so the tape still does its job.

What should go on a tradie business card?

Your name, trade, phone, email and the area you service, plus your licence number where your trade needs it on advertising. A QR code to your Google reviews works well on the back.

Which suburbs should I letterbox?

Start with the suburbs you can reach quickly and want more work in. Many trades pick two or three suburbs close to home and repeat the drop every few months, so the name becomes familiar.

Can you match everything to our branding?

Yes. Workwear, cards, invoice books, magnets, flyers and signs all come from the same artwork, so the business looks the same on the ute, on the shirt and in the letterbox.

How long do orders take?

Print depends on the product, and we give you an honest date before you order. Embroidered workwear takes ten to fifteen business days from artwork approval as a guide, plus freight.

Ready to kit out the team?

Send us the headcount, the garments you like and your deadline. We will come back with a real number, including freight to your address.
