Real jobs for real organisations

ONE SUPPLIER.
EVERY PIECE ON BRAND.

Three organisations that use us for more than one thing. Menus and cards for a national franchise, signage and flags for a growing church, and every range we offer for a local charity.

Franchise · Hospitality

Xpresso Mobile Café: 30 locations, one look

Xpresso Mobile Café is a national mobile coffee franchise. Every franchisee needs the same menus and cards, with their own name, area and phone number on them.

Since 2024 we have looked after that for around 30 Xpresso locations across Queensland, New South Wales, Victoria and Western Australia. We keep the master artwork on file, set up each new franchisee's details, send a proof, then print and deliver straight to them.

The result is a franchise that looks the same in Ipswich as it does in Footscray, without head office managing a printer for every new van. Embroidered uniforms are the next part of the brand we are taking on.

What we make for them

  • A4 tri-fold menus, set up for each location
  • Flyer and menu brochures
  • Business cards with a loyalty card on the back
  • Matching email signatures for each franchisee
  • Embroidered uniforms, starting now

Church · Café · Events

Sanctuary Church: a café, a youth ministry and a conference

Sanctuary Church at Mons on the Sunshine Coast runs much more than Sunday services. There is Sanctuary Café, a youth ministry called Sanctuary Youth, special Sundays through the year, and a conference.

Each of those has its own look, and each needs signs, flags and print that hold up outside and on stage. We produce them from the church's artwork, check every file before it prints, and deliver on the Sunshine Coast ourselves.

What we make for them

  • A-frame signs for Sanctuary Café
  • Café and playground flags
  • Sanctuary Youth bow banners
  • A stretch fabric media wall for the 2026 conference
  • Coffee loyalty cards, DL cards and welcome pack folders

Church · Charity · Community

Rhythm: every range, one supplier

Please note: Rhythm is our founder's own church and community charity. We show it because it uses every range we offer, not as an independent review.

Rhythm is a church and community charity at Baringa on the Sunshine Coast. It runs services, community programs and events, and it needs to reach new neighbours as the area grows.

That means letterbox flyers to bring people in, corflute signs and banners on the day, flags for outdoor events, cards for the team, and branded merch and apparel for volunteers. It all comes from us, with one proof process and one invoice.

What we make for them

  • Letterbox drop flyers
  • Corflute signs and canvas banners
  • Pull-up banners and teardrop flags
  • Business cards and a community brochure
  • Promotional items, stickers and apparel
