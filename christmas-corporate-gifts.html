Christmas gift questions

When should I order Christmas gifts?

As early as you can. Decoration, proofing and freight all take time and December gets busy for every supplier. Send your numbers now and we will confirm timing on your quote before you commit.

Is there a minimum order?

Minimums depend on the product and the decoration. Many gifts start from small runs. Each product page shows the price breaks, and your quote spells out the minimum for your choice.

Can you put our logo on any of these?

Yes. Every gift on this page can carry your logo by print, engraving, embroidery or deboss, depending on the product. You approve a proof before anything is made.

Do the prices include our logo?

The from prices are per unit before decoration. Open any product to price your own quantity with setup included, or ask us for a quote on a mix of gifts.

Can we mix different gifts for different clients?

Yes. Plenty of businesses give premium gifts to their top clients and a simpler gift to everyone else. Tell us your list and budget and we will put options together.
