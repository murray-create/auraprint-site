Custom promotional fridge magnets in full colour. Commercial grade indoor magnet, high gloss sealed, free die cut. Pick your style and size for a live price.

  • ▸ Material: commercial grade indoor magnet
  • ▸ Finish: high gloss, sealed with a protective film
  • ▸ Print: CMYK digital full colour HD quality
  • ▸ Die cut: free cutting forme setup, no die fees

Why print with Aura?

Every job is preflight-checked by a human, and you approve a digital proof before anything prints. Australian made magnets, delivered Australia-wide with a flat rate per job, or pick up in Baringa.

Frequently asked questions

What size fridge magnets can I order?

From small 47x47mm through business card, A7, A6, DL and up to A3 whiteboard magnets. Pick your style and size and the price updates live.

Are the magnets Australian made?

Yes. Commercial grade indoor magnet, high gloss sealed, CMYK full colour, Australian made and owned, with free die cutting.

How is delivery charged?

A flat delivery rate per job, Australia-wide. We are Sunshine Coast based, so local pick up in Baringa is welcome too.
