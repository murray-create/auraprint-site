What branded coasters cost

A real price you can hold us to

250 round cork coasters with a laser engraved logo works out at $405 plus GST, which is $1.62 each with setup included. 100 single slate coasters, laser engraved, is $346 plus GST, or $3.46 each. 100 square bamboo coasters with a pad printed logo is $407 plus GST, or $4.07 each. A full colour glass coaster is $570 plus GST for 100, which is $5.70 each. Every product here has its own calculator, so put in your own quantity rather than guessing from a range.

Cork, bamboo, glass or slate

Cork is the low cost choice. It is light, it soaks up drips, and it suits big handouts, cafes and mail outs. Bamboo looks natural and sits well in an office or a real estate gift pack. Glass and slate feel heavy and premium, so they read as a real gift for clients rather than a freebie. Ceramic coasters take a full colour photo print. PVC coasters are moulded to your own shape and colours, and they last for years on a bar. If you want cheap full colour board coasters for a pub or event, see our printed cardboard coasters instead.

How the logo goes on

Cork, bamboo and slate are usually laser engraved. Engraving is permanent and looks crisp, but it shows as a burnt or etched tone rather than your brand colour. Pad printing puts one or two solid colours on bamboo and glass. Direct digital and sublimation print in full colour, so photos, gradients and patterns all work. Glass can also take an imitation etch for a frosted look. The decoration options for each product are listed in its calculator.

Sets, minimums and stock

Single cork coasters start at 250 units. Most glass, bamboo and slate coasters start at 100. Boxed sets of two or four start at 25 to 100 sets, and they make a tidy client gift. Bar runners start from 10 to 25. Each product shows its first price break and its current supplier stock, and many of the cork and glass lines hold tens of thousands in stock.

Standard production is about seven to ten working days after approval, plus freight. Tell us your deadline up front and we will only quote what can make it.

Artwork for promotional products

Send a vector file: EPS, AI or PDF with the fonts outlined. A JPG or PNG taken off a website cannot be screen printed or engraved cleanly at product size. Send your brand colours as Pantone references too, because decoration is colour matched to Pantone rather than CMYK, and a colour that looks right on screen will not necessarily mix the same in ink.

Decoration areas are smaller than people expect. If your logo has fine detail, small type or a tagline, it may need simplifying to survive at 35mm wide. We will tell you before you commit, and you will see it on the mockup at actual size. If all you have is a low resolution logo, say so and we will redraw it.

Frequently asked questions

How much are custom coasters?

Cork coasters with a laser engraved logo start around $1.60 each at 250 units, setup included. Bamboo and slate coasters are around $3.50 to $4 each at 100, and full colour glass coasters are around $5 to $6 each at 100. Use the calculator on any product here for a real figure on your quantity.

What is the minimum order on coasters?

It depends on the product. Single cork coasters start at 250 units and some board coasters start at 500. Most glass, bamboo and slate coasters start at 100. Boxed sets and bar runners can start from 10 to 25. The first price break is shown on every product.

Can you print a full colour logo or photo on a coaster?

Yes. Ceramic, glass and some bamboo coasters can be printed in full colour with direct digital or sublimation print. Cork can take a full colour transfer. Laser engraving gives one tone only, but it never wears off.

What is the difference between these and your printed cardboard coasters?

The coasters on this page are reusable gifts and giveaways made from cork, bamboo, glass, slate, ceramic and PVC. Our printed cardboard coasters are thick board coasters printed in full colour, like the ones you see in a pub. They cost less in large runs and are made for single use. Both can carry your logo.

Which coaster is best for a client gift?

A boxed set of slate, glass or bamboo coasters. They feel solid, they come presented in sets of two or four, and they stay on a coffee table for years. Cork is better for big handouts where the cost per head matters most.

Are there eco friendly coasters?

Yes. Cork, bamboo and slate coasters are all natural materials, and many are part of our eco range. Use the Eco filter above the products to see them.

How long do branded coasters take?

Standard production is about seven to ten working days after you approve the mockup, plus freight. Lead times vary by product and decoration. Your quote confirms the timing for your job before you approve anything.
