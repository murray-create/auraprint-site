What a full Maleny & Hinterland drop costs

Covering every residential letterbox in the area means 4,980 letterboxes. At 47.6c per letterbox delivered, that is about $2,373 including GST.

Printing is quoted on top. A DL or A5 flyer on 150gsm is the cheapest thing to both print and deliver, because it stays inside Australia Post's small letter weight band.

Letterbox drops in Maleny & the Hinterland

The Blackall Range runs from Maleny through Montville and Flaxton up to Mapleton, with Conondale, Kenilworth and the Obi Obi valley behind it. Populations are small, properties are large, and the driveways are long.

The Maleny & the Hinterland area is 25 suburbs and 4,980 residential letterboxes, which is about 4% of every letterbox on the Sunshine Coast. Maleny is the largest single suburb in it at 1,344 letterboxes, so it is usually where a first test drop goes.

Who drops flyers here

Tourism operators and accommodation, galleries and cafes on the range, rural and land services, vets and produce merchants, and community events.

Why we book it through Australia Post

This is the area where walker distribution simply does not happen. The homes are too far apart to walk and the roads are too winding to drive quickly. Australia Post already runs a mail contract down every one of these roads, so a hinterland drop costs you the same per letterbox as a beachfront one.

Every run is lodged as Australia Post Unaddressed Mail. You nominate a delivery week, we print the flyer on a stock that sits in the right weight band, bundle it to Australia Post specification, lodge the job and you get the lodgement receipt. There is no walker to skip a street and no report to take on trust.

Sizing the run

Ask for roadside delivery points to be included. In this area they are most of the audience, not a rounding error.

Frequently asked

How many letterboxes are there in Maleny & the Hinterland? There are 4,980 residential letterboxes and 263 business letterboxes across the 25 suburbs in the Maleny & the Hinterland area that accept unaddressed advertising material. The largest single suburb is Maleny with 1,344 residential letterboxes. These are Australia Post delivery point counts, extracted 25 August 2026.

What does a letterbox drop across Maleny & Hinterland cost? Covering every residential letterbox in the area is 4,980 letterboxes. At 47.6 cents per letterbox delivered, GST included, that is about $2,373. Printing is quoted on top. Delivery-only jobs carry a minimum charge of $250. It is waived when we print the flyer as well. You do not have to take the whole area: pick the suburbs you want and the quantity follows.

Can I book just one suburb in Maleny & Hinterland? Yes. Australia Post lets us book by locality, so you can take a single suburb or any combination of them. For a smaller area than that, tell us the streets or the estate in the booking notes and we size the run to match.

How long does it take? Allow three to five business days to print, then the run is lodged for a nominated Australia Post delivery week. Bookings generally need to be in about two weeks ahead of the week you want.

Not your area?

We book Australia Post Unaddressed Mail to any postcode in Australia, not just the Sunshine Coast. Same posties, same delivery week, same official letterbox counts. Tell us the suburbs or the postcodes and we will come back with the numbers.
