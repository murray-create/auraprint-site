What branded tech costs

A real worked example

100 USB Type C earphones with a one colour print come to $539 plus GST, which is $5.39 each including setup. Power banks typically land between $12 and $40 each at 100 units depending on capacity. Bluetooth speakers start around $15 and climb quickly. Every product here has a calculator so you can price your own quantity.

Capacity is the price

On a power bank, the milliamp hour rating drives nearly all of the cost. A 5,000mAh bank charges a phone once and is a sensible giveaway. A 20,000mAh bank charges a phone four times and is a genuine gift, at roughly three times the price. Buy the capacity that matches the recipient rather than the one that hits a budget.

Wireless charging and cables

Wireless charging pads and 3-in-1 charging stands have taken over from USB drives as the standard office tech gift. Multi-tip cables and cable organisers are the cheap end of the same idea and they get used daily. All of them give you a flat surface for a logo, which tech items often do not.

Decoration on tech

Most tech items are pad printed or laser engraved. Engraving on aluminium and metal casings looks premium and is permanent. Print holds your brand colour but can wear on an item handled every day. Print areas on tech are usually small, so simplify the logo and drop the tagline.

What the Coast uses branded tech for

Client gifts and referrals

A power bank with your logo is the gift a client keeps in the car and sees every week.

Conferences and speakers

Speaker gifts and VIP satchels, where a tech item separates the premium tier from the general one.

Staff recognition

Length of service and end of year gifts, where the budget justifies a speaker or a larger power bank.

Real estate and finance

High value client thank-yous at settlement, where the gift needs to feel proportionate to the transaction.

Trades and field teams

Rugged power banks and multi-tip cables for crews who are away from a power point all day.

Tourism operators

Charging accessories for guests, branded and left in rooms or handed out on tours.

Artwork for promotional products

Send a vector file: EPS, AI or PDF with the fonts outlined. A JPG or PNG taken off a website cannot be screen printed or engraved cleanly at product size. Send your brand colours as Pantone references too, because decoration is colour matched to Pantone rather than CMYK, and a colour that looks right on screen will not necessarily mix the same in ink.

Decoration areas are smaller than people expect. If your logo has fine detail, small type or a tagline, it may need simplifying to survive at 35mm wide. We will tell you before you commit, and you will see it on the mockup at actual size. If all you have is a low resolution logo, say so and we will redraw it.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a branded power bank?

Usually $12 to $40 each at 100 units including decoration, depending on capacity. A 5,000mAh bank sits at the low end, a 20,000mAh bank at the top. The calculator on each product gives you the real number.

What capacity power bank should I buy?

Five thousand milliamp hours for a giveaway, which is one full phone charge. Ten thousand for a solid client gift. Twenty thousand when it is a premium gift and you want it treated as one.

Can you engrave a logo on tech items?

Yes, on metal and aluminium casings. Engraving is permanent and looks premium, and it shows the metal underneath rather than your brand colour. Printing keeps the colour but wears faster on a daily-use item.

Do branded tech items come with a warranty?

Electronic items carry the supplier warranty, and the term varies by product. If you are buying a large run of a higher value item, ask us and we will confirm the warranty in writing before you order.

Are USB drives still worth buying?

Less than they were. Most people now move files through cloud storage, and some workplaces block USB devices outright. Charging accessories and audio have largely replaced them as the default tech gift.

What is the minimum order on tech items?

Usually 25 to 100 units depending on the value of the item. Higher value items have lower minimums.

How long does branded tech take?

Around seven to ten working days for standard production after artwork approval, plus freight. Tech items are more likely than most to be made to order, so check the stock figure on the product before you commit to a date.
