Letterbox drops in Kawana

Kawana runs from the canal homes at Minyama and Parrearra down through Buddina and Warana to the newer medium density at Birtinya and Bokarina. Mountain Creek sits behind it and is one of the largest single residential pockets on the coast.

The Kawana area is 8 suburbs and 11,201 residential letterboxes, which is about 9% of every letterbox on the Sunshine Coast. Mountain Creek is the largest single suburb in it at 3,144 letterboxes, so it is usually where a first test drop goes.

Who drops flyers here

Allied health and specialists around the Sunshine Coast University Hospital, gyms and swim schools, marine and boating trades, and the light industrial businesses along Nicklin Way at Warana.

Why we book it through Australia Post

Birtinya has grown a dense apartment core around the hospital precinct, and the canal estates at Minyama and Parrearra have long private driveways. Australia Post covers both without argument, and the delivery week is fixed so you can time a drop to an opening or an event.

Every run is lodged as Australia Post Unaddressed Mail. You nominate a delivery week, we print the flyer on a stock that sits in the right weight band, bundle it to Australia Post specification, lodge the job and you get the lodgement receipt. There is no walker to skip a street and no report to take on trust.

Sizing the run

Warana carries an unusually high number of business letterboxes for its size. If you sell to trades rather than households, ask us to include business delivery points in the Warana leg.

Frequently asked

How many letterboxes are there in Kawana? There are 11,201 residential letterboxes and 784 business letterboxes across the 8 suburbs in the Kawana area that accept unaddressed advertising material. The largest single suburb is Mountain Creek with 3,144 residential letterboxes. These are Australia Post delivery point counts, extracted 25 August 2026.

What does a letterbox drop across Kawana cost? Covering every residential letterbox in the area is 11,201 letterboxes. At 47.6 cents per letterbox delivered, GST included, that is about $5,337. Printing is quoted on top. Delivery-only jobs carry a minimum charge of $250. It is waived when we print the flyer as well. You do not have to take the whole area: pick the suburbs you want and the quantity follows.

Can I book just one suburb in Kawana? Yes. Australia Post lets us book by locality, so you can take a single suburb or any combination of them. For a smaller area than that, tell us the streets or the estate in the booking notes and we size the run to match.

How long does it take? Allow three to five business days to print, then the run is lodged for a nominated Australia Post delivery week. Bookings generally need to be in about two weeks ahead of the week you want.

Not your area?

We book Australia Post Unaddressed Mail to any postcode in Australia, not just the Sunshine Coast. Same posties, same delivery week, same official letterbox counts. Tell us the suburbs or the postcodes and we will come back with the numbers.
