Why print with Aura?

Every job is preflight-checked by a human, and you approve a digital proof before anything prints. Delivered anywhere in Australia, and often dropped off in person if you are local to the Sunshine Coast.

Frequently asked questions

What is NV Velvet?

NV Velvet is a luxurious soft-touch laminate over thick 450gsm artboard. It feels like suede and makes colours pop. Add Spot UV for glossy highlights.

How fast can I get my cards?

Standard turnaround is 3-5 business days plus delivery. Faster production is available on this product - select your speed above and we will confirm the real date before you commit.

Do I see a proof before printing?

Always. A digital proof lands in your inbox and nothing prints until you approve it.
