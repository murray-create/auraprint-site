A real Baringa job: Sunshine Dent Co

Sunshine Dent Co is a Baringa business. They ordered matching uniforms for the team across five garment styles.

83 garments in one order

40 JB’s Wear Podium contrast polos in black and gold, 31 Winning Spirit classic pique polos in black and white, 6 Workcraft yellow polos and 6 black hoodies.

The same logo on every garment

Each garment has the logo on the left chest and a large print across the upper back. Using one artwork setup across every style keeps the team looking the same.

DTF across every style

We used DTF transfers. There are no screens to make, so mixing several garment styles in one order did not add setup costs.

More than shirts

The same business also uses us for corflute signs and other printing, so the brand matches across uniforms and signs.

T-shirt printing in Caloundra

We print custom t-shirts for Caloundra businesses, clubs, schools, churches and events. Staff tees, event and volunteer shirts, fundraising runs and merch you sell all start the same way. You pick the tee, send us your logo, and we send you a mockup to approve.

Small runs and full colour designs are printed by DTF, which we press in-house. There are no screens to make, so ten shirts or a different name on every shirt makes sense. Bigger runs of one or two colour designs are screen printed, which gets cheaper per shirt as the numbers go up. We tell you which method suits your job and why.

Tees come from AS Colour for retail quality merch and JB’s Wear for volume orders with a wide size run. Every tee shows its price online, and we deliver Caloundra orders ourselves. See our full guide to t-shirt printing on the Sunshine Coast, or shop tees with prices. For signs, cards, menus and other printing, see printing in Caloundra.

How ordering works

Tell us what you need

Pick the garments below and send them through, or just describe the job. Headcount, rough sizes, how many logo positions and your deadline are enough to start.

We quote it properly

We price the garment, the decoration and the freight to your address as one number, and we confirm the real minimum order and lead time for your job rather than a general one.

Approve your mockup

You get a visual showing your logo in position and at actual size on the garment you chose. Nothing is stitched or pressed until you approve it.

We produce and deliver

As a guide, ten to fifteen business days from artwork approval, plus freight. The clock starts at approval, not at order, and supplier stock is the usual cause of a delay.

Frequently asked questions

Do you do t-shirt printing in Caloundra?

Yes. We print custom t-shirts for Caloundra businesses, clubs and events, by DTF for small runs and full colour designs, and by screen printing for bigger runs. We are based in Baringa and we deliver Caloundra orders ourselves.

Do you have a uniform shop in Caloundra?

We are based in Baringa at Caloundra West, and we sell online. You can browse every garment with prices on our website. We meet customers face to face when a job needs it, and we deliver Caloundra orders ourselves.

Can I see the garment prices before I ask for a quote?

Yes. Every style in our shop shows its price inc GST, with bulk savings from 10 pieces. Your logo is quoted on top once we have seen the artwork.

Which logo method should I choose?

Embroidery suits polos, caps and business shirts that need to look like a uniform. DTF suits full colour logos, small runs and names. Screen printing suits big runs of simple designs. We will tell you which we would pick for your job.

Can you add staff names?

Yes. Names are usually added by DTF on the chest. Send us the list with sizes when you ask for a quote.

How long does a uniform order take?

As a guide, 10 to 15 business days from when you approve the artwork. If you have a start date or an event, tell us up front.

Can you decorate shirts we already own?

Often, yes. Tell us the garment and fabric, and we will confirm it suits the method before you commit.

Ready to kit out the team?

Send us the headcount, the garments you like and your deadline. We will come back with a real number, including freight to your address.
